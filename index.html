Good questions

Frequently asked

Quick, clear answers, the kind people (and search engines) are actually looking for.

What is person-centered disability consulting?

Person-centered disability consulting helps organizations design programs around a person's strengths, rights, and goals rather than their diagnosis or deficits, which makes services more dignified, individualized, and cost-effective.

What does a functional nutritionist do for people with disabilities or chronic illness?

A functional nutritionist provides a non-medical assessment of what's happening in your body and uses food, not supplements, to help you feel stronger and more energetic. The focus is wellbeing and managing chronic conditions like POTS, not weight loss.

How can I get help navigating regional center or developmental services?

Family support and disability consulting offers one-on-one guidance and peer support to help families understand and navigate regional and developmental services, translating early intervention plans into real, everyday life.

What is body image coaching?

Body image coaching takes a body-neutral approach that helps you nourish and care for your body and build a steadier, kinder relationship with yourself, moving beyond appearance and the pressure to change how you look.
