About the speaker

Lara Minges

Lara is a disability consultant, functional nutritionist, and coach with 20+ years at the intersection of disability, early childhood, and family life. She helps organizations build person-centered, rights-focused programs.

She serves on boards and committees including Out of Site Ministries, the Regional Center Vendor Advisory Committee, Hand in Hand, IHSS, and PASC. Lara lives with POTS and brings that lived experience, and a warm, practical style, to every room she is in.
