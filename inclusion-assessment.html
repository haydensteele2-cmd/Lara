What this assessment looks at

Lara Minges helps organizations build person-centered, rights-based programs. This assessment covers six areas that matter most:

  • Voice & choice: whether the people you serve shape services and lead their own plans.
  • Access & environment: how easy it is to take part, get started, and ask for what you need.
  • Culture & language: how staff learn about disability and how you talk about the people you serve.
  • Leadership & partnerships: who holds decision-making power, and how you work with disability-led organizations.
  • Workforce & employment: how you support direct-support staff and hire and promote employees with disabilities.
  • Measuring & feedback: how you define success and learn from the people you serve.
