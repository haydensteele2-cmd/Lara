What this assessment looks at

Lara Minges helps organizations move from a deficit-based model to a person-centered, rights-based one. This assessment covers four areas that matter most:

  • Voice & choice: whether the people you serve shape the services they use.
  • Access & environment: how easy it is to take part, and how requests are handled.
  • Culture & leadership: training, language, and who holds decision-making roles.
  • Measuring & feedback: how you define success and learn from the people you serve.
