Program Development & Growth Support

Consulting that helps disability organizations build person-centered, rights-focused, and cost-effective programs.

By Lara Minges · Updated October 14, 2026

Book a consult See all services

Disability Intersection partners with organizations that want to serve people with disabilities better, and more sustainably. The work centers on one shift: building services around a person-centered approach (who a person is, what they want, and the rights they hold).

This is hands-on consulting for teams who know something needs to change but want a clear, practical path to get there. Lara helps you redesign programs around the individual, ground your services in disability rights, and do it in a way that's realistic and cost-effective, with a plan your team will use.

Good questions

Frequently asked

Quick, clear answers about how this work actually goes.

What is person-centered program development?

It's designing services around a person's strengths, rights, and goals, so support is individualized, dignified, and effective.

How is this different from standard disability services consulting?

The focus is on building a person-centered, rights-based model and doing it cost-effectively.

Who is this for?

Disability-serving organizations, nonprofits, and agencies that want their programs to be more individualized and sustainable.
