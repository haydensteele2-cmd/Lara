Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Disability Intersection ("we," "us," or "our"), led by Lara Minges in Sierra Madre, California, collects, uses, and protects information when you visit disabilityintersection.org (the "Site") or contact us through it. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us. When you use the Let's Talk form or email us, we receive the details you choose to share, such as your name, email address, the topic you want to discuss, a message, and when you are generally available.

Scheduling. If you book a call through an online scheduling tool, we receive the information you enter there, such as your name, email address, the time you choose, any notes you add, and details needed to hold the call (for example, a video link).

Newsletter. If you subscribe to our email newsletter, we collect your email address and, if you provide it, your name. We send you the newsletter only if you sign up, and every email includes a way to unsubscribe.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you view, and the date and time. We use this only to keep the Site running securely and reliably.

Accessibility preferences. If you use the accessibility toolbar (the "Aa" button), your choices, such as text size or contrast, are saved in your own browser so they carry across pages. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Analytics, cookies, and similar technologies. We may use website analytics, such as Google Analytics or a privacy-focused alternative, to understand how visitors use the Site (for example, which pages are viewed, how visitors arrive, and the general region, device, and browser they use). Analytics tools may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect your IP address. We use this information in aggregate to improve the Site. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels to follow you around the web. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, and many browsers and extensions also let you opt out of analytics.

2. Please do not send sensitive health information

Our contact form is not a secure channel for medical or other highly sensitive information. Please do not include diagnoses, medical records, or similar details in a form submission or email. If you choose to share them anyway, we will use them only to respond to you. Our services are educational and non-medical; see our Terms of Service · Privacy Policy.

3. How we use information

  • To reply to your questions and requests, including arranging a free consult call.
  • To schedule and hold calls and sessions.
  • To send you our newsletter and occasional updates, if you have signed up.
  • To understand how the Site is used and make it better.
  • To provide, personalize, and improve our consulting, coaching, nutrition, family support, and early-childhood services.
  • To keep the Site secure and prevent spam and abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations and keep reasonable business records.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising purposes.

4. Who we share information with

We share information only with service providers who help us operate the Site and respond to you, and only as needed:

  • Website hosting and form processing. Our hosting provider, Netlify, delivers the Site and receives and stores contact form submissions, then sends them to us by email.
  • Email. Messages you send us, and notifications of form submissions, are received through our email provider.
  • Scheduling and video calls. If you book a call, the scheduling tool we use (and the calendar and video-meeting services connected to it) process the details you enter in order to book and hold the call.
  • Newsletter. If you subscribe, our email newsletter provider stores your email address and sends the emails on our behalf.
  • Analytics. If we use an analytics service, it receives information about your visit, as described above.
  • Fonts. The Site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers, which can see your IP address when the fonts load.

The providers we use have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to read them. We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or safety or that of others, or in connection with a sale or transfer of the business. Links on the Site to other websites are governed by those sites' own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep information

We keep contact form submissions, scheduling details, and emails for as long as needed to respond to you and for reasonable business records. We keep newsletter subscriptions until you unsubscribe. Analytics data is kept in aggregate for a reasonable period. You may ask us to delete your information at any time (see "Your choices and rights" below).

6. Your choices and rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. To make a request, email us at the address below.

California residents. Under California privacy laws, you may have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request deletion or correction of it; and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by California law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

Do Not Track. We do not track you across other websites, so there is nothing to opt out of in that respect.

7. Children's privacy

Some of our services, such as Musikgarten classes, are for young children, but they are arranged through parents and caregivers. The Site is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, including encrypted (HTTPS) connections to the Site. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, including when we add or change the tools we use. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Continued use of the Site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions or requests about this policy or your information? Email disabilityintersection@startmail.com.
