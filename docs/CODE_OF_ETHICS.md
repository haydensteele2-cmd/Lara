# Disability Intersection: Code of Ethics

**Status:** DRAFT for Lara Minges's review and approval. Last updated October 18, 2026.

Five commitments guide everything we publish and offer: the website, blog posts, assessments, talks, social media, and services.

## 1. We are research-backed

- Every fact, statistic, and health, legal, or program claim carries a **citation** to a reliable source, such as a government agency, peer-reviewed research, or a recognized professional or standards body.
- Statistics link to a **primary source** we have opened and read. When we can't verify a number, we leave it out.
- We note the **date** each source was checked, because rules and programs change.
- Lara's experience and opinions are welcome and labeled as hers ("In my experience..."). Research is labeled as research.

## 2. We share accurate information

- When we're unsure, we say so.
- Quotes, sources, testimonials, results, and credentials are always real.
- We name partners, features, and clients with their permission and describe them truthfully.
- When we find an error, we **correct it promptly** and note the correction.
- Health-related and legal-adjacent content is **reviewed before publishing**, and we describe our services clearly: educational, non-medical, and general information.

## 3. We create an encouraging, supportive environment for everyone

- Our tone is warm, respectful, and hopeful, and we meet people where they are.
- We treat every person, body, and choice with respect.
- We center people's own goals and strengths and honor their right to make their own decisions.

## 4. We inspire with honesty and hope

- We describe what we offer clearly and truthfully, and people decide whether it is the right fit.
- We share risks at their real size and keep the focus on what is possible.
- We invite people to take the next step when they are ready, on their own timeline.

## 5. We are as inclusive as possible

- **Language:** We use person-first language ("people with disabilities") by default and follow each person's own preference when we know it.
- **Access:** Our website, documents, talks, and materials are designed to be accessible, with alt text, captions, plain language, and readable design. We welcome feedback and fix barriers.
- **Representation:** We show a wide range of people and experiences and tell many different stories.
- **Privacy and consent:** We share people's stories and images with their clear permission.

## How we hold ourselves to this

1. Every public post, page, or assessment gets **Lara's approval** before it goes live.
2. Health- and legal-adjacent content gets an additional review, ideally by a qualified professional.
3. Informational content has a **source list** and a "last reviewed" date.
4. Anyone can tell us about an error or an access barrier at disabilityintersection@startmail.com, and we respond.
