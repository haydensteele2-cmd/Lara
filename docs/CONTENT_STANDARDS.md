# Disability Intersection: Content Standards

**Status:** DRAFT. Follows the Code of Ethics (see CODE_OF_ETHICS.md). Last updated October 18, 2026.

## Voice
- Posts are written in **Lara's first-person voice** ("I", "my clients"), and **Lara approves every post** before it is published.
- Personal stories (including living with POTS) are shared only as Lara chooses, and never identify clients without permission.
- Warm, plain language. Short sentences. No jargon without explanation. No fear, no shaming.

## Language
- Default to **person-first language**: "people with disabilities," "children with disabilities," "a person with a disability."
- Follow an individual's own preference when we know it, and quote people using their own words.
- "Disability community" and "disability-led organizations" are fine as community terms.
- Avoid outdated or demeaning terms, and avoid "inspirational" or "suffering from" framing.

## Citations (required)
- **Every fact, statistic, program rule, or health or legal claim gets a numbered citation** at the end of the post, with: title, publisher, link, and the date we checked it.
- Prefer primary and official sources (government agencies, peer-reviewed research, recognized standards bodies).
- Never cite a statistic we haven't opened and read. If we can't find a source, we reword it as Lara's experience or leave it out.
- Flag secondary sources (blogs, vendor sites) as such, and use them only for definitions or context.
- Rules and programs change. Posts with factual content show a "last reviewed" date and are re-checked at least twice a year.

## Health and legal topics
- Clear note on every health-related post: educational, non-medical, not a substitute for care from a licensed professional.
- Clear note on legal-adjacent posts: general information, not legal advice.
- No cure, treatment, or guaranteed-result claims. No weight-loss promises.
- Extra review before publishing (Lara, and a qualified professional where possible).

## Structure (SEO and AI answers)
1. Question-style title.
2. A direct answer in the first 40 to 60 words.
3. Short sections with clear headings, lists where helpful.
4. 3 FAQs that match the page's FAQ markup.
5. Links to the relevant service page.
6. Sources list with dates.

## Publishing checklist
- [ ] Facts checked and cited with links and dates
- [ ] No fear-based or shaming language
- [ ] "People with disabilities" language used
- [ ] Health/legal disclaimers added where needed
- [ ] Images have alt text; contrast and reading level checked
- [ ] Lara has read and approved the final version
- [ ] Title, description, and byline are set

Primary services to focus on: **Program Development & Growth Support**, **Body Image Coaching**, and **Functional Nutrition**. Other services follow.
