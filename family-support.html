Family Support & Disability Consulting

One-on-one guidance and peer support for families navigating regional and developmental services, from early intervention to everyday life.

Book a consult See all services

If you're a parent working with the Department of Developmental Services or your regional center, you already know how much there is to navigate. Disability Intersection offers one-on-one consulting and peer support to help you understand your child's development, make sense of the system, and feel less alone in it.

A lot of families hit the same wall: early intervention ends, and suddenly the structured support falls away just as real life gets more complicated. Lara helps you translate what you learned in early intervention into everyday life, and gives you a steady, knowledgeable person to think things through with.

The disability-positive approach

Good questions

Frequently asked

Quick, clear answers about how this work actually goes.

How do I navigate the regional center or developmental services system?

A family support consultant can walk you through how the programs work, what your child is entitled to, and how to make it function in everyday life.

What happens after early intervention ends?

Family support helps you translate early-intervention goals into ongoing, real-life routines so progress doesn't stall.

What does "disability-positive" support mean?

It means looking at your child as a whole person and building support around their strengths and needs, rather than focusing only on deficits.
