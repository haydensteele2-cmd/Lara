Body Image Coaching

Gentle, body-neutral coaching for anyone who wants to nourish and care for their body and build a kinder relationship with themselves, beyond appearance.

By Lara Minges · Updated October 14, 2026

Start a conversation See all services

Body image coaching at Disability Intersection takes a body-neutral approach. Instead of chasing "loving how you look," the work focuses on something steadier and more sustainable: caring for your body, making sure you're eating and looking after yourself, and easing the constant pressure to change how you appear.

Lara meets you where you are with warmth and zero judgment. Together you build practical, compassionate habits and a kinder inner voice, so your relationship with your body becomes calmer, more neutral, and more grounded in how you actually live.

Good questions

Frequently asked

Quick, clear answers, starting with the one people ask most.

Is body image coaching only for people with disabilities?

No. This coaching is for any adult or older youth who wants a steadier, more compassionate relationship with their body, disability or not. Lara's broader practice is rooted in disability advocacy, but body-neutral coaching welcomes everyone equally.

What does "body neutrality" mean?

Body neutrality is about respecting and caring for your body as it is, rather than requiring yourself to love how it looks.

Is this a diet or weight-loss program?

No. The focus is self-care, nourishment, and a kinder relationship with your body, not dieting or weight loss.

Body image coaching is educational and supportive, not therapy or medical treatment. If you are struggling with an eating disorder or your mental health, please reach out to a licensed professional.
